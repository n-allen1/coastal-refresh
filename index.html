A Fresh Look Without a Full Renovation.

Going Coastal Refresh Co. helps Gulf Shores, Orange Beach and Fort Morgan property owners refresh and update their beach homes and condos without the expense and disruption of a full renovation. We work with what you already have whenever possible, adding smart, affordable updates — from paint, lighting and decor to feature walls, bathroom refreshes, custom storage and creative spaces. Whether you're refreshing for yourself, attracting renters or preparing a property to sell, our goal is to make your space feel fresh, inviting and coastal — while keeping your budget in mind.

Our Story

How Going Coastal Refresh Co Began

Hands-on. Experienced. Results that matter.

What started with refreshing our own homes and beach properties has grown into something we truly enjoy doing together.

Since 2019, we've owned, updated and transformed condos and beach properties along the Alabama Gulf Coast — learning firsthand which improvements make the biggest difference without requiring a complete renovation.

We're hands-on from start to finish. We paint, repurpose furniture, tackle feature walls, update fixtures, rethink underused spaces, shop for budget-friendly finds and work with trusted local professionals when a project calls for them.

Our approach has always been simple: use what works, improve what doesn't, and spend money where it will have the greatest impact.

The properties we've refreshed have consistently stood out in their buildings and helped us achieve strong resale results and increased market appeal. That experience taught us how much thoughtful, cost-conscious updates can change the way a property looks, photographs, rents and sells.

Going Coastal Refresh Co. grew naturally from what we were already doing — and loving. Now we're bringing that same owner's perspective, hands-on experience and eye for value to other Gulf Shores, Orange Beach and Fort Morgan property owners who want a fresh look without the cost and disruption of a full renovation.

Refresh what you have. Repurpose where you can. Invest where it counts.

— Dave & Donna Kuhn, Going Coastal Refresh Co.
