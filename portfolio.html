Recent Transformations

Coastal Condo Refresh

Complete makeover of a beach condo, transforming outdated decor and fixtures into a modern, rental-ready space. Features include updated bathrooms, a refreshed kitchen, and new coastal-themed furnishings to maximize rental appeal and guest satisfaction.

Bathroom Renovation

Modernization of a dated beach condo bathroom. The renovation includes new tile work, updated vanity and fixtures, improved lighting, and a more functional layout to create a spa-like experience for renters.

Bedroom Suite Sanctuary

A serene bedroom update prioritizing comfort and style for a beach home. This project involved a complete refresh with a new color palette, plush bedding, enhanced lighting, and a custom-designed storage bed for optimal space utilization.

Rental Property Prep

Strategic improvements for a property preparing to enter the rental market. Focus on high-impact, affordable updates like fresh paint, new window treatments, and durable, attractive decor to immediately enhance curb appeal and rental readiness.

Realtor Listing Refresh

A quick and efficient property refresh for a Realtor's listing. This project involved light cosmetic updates, decluttering, and strategic staging to make the property more appealing to potential buyers and reduce time on the market.

Vacation Rental Boost

Enhancement project for a vacation rental property focused on increasing booking potential. Key updates include painting, new lighting, durable flooring, and custom storage solutions designed for frequent guest turnover.
